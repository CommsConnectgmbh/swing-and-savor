How it works

Three steps to a live match.

Sign in

8-digit code by email. No password, no magic link. Profile with handle, HCP and home course.

Cup or duel

Spin up a cup tournament with Team A/B, or challenge a friend to Match Play directly.

Play live

Tap in your strokes hole by hole. Score, hole winners and remaining holes compute themselves.

Cups, leaderboards, photos from the course and the 19th-hole talk — all in our Discord. Live results post automatically when a cup ends.
