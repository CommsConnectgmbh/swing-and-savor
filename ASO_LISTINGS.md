### Long description (App Store + Play, ~3000 chars)
```
Swing & Savor turns your weekend round into a proper tournament.

Build Ryder-Cup-style cup brackets, challenge friends to head-to-head Match Play and watch every hole come in live — no paper scorecards, no debate about where you stand.

▸ MATCH PLAY DONE RIGHT
Singles or 2 v 2 Doubles. Handicap strokes computed automatically, dormie logic baked in, hole winners locked before the next tee box. You enter the strokes — the app does the rest.

▸ CUP BRACKETS
Create your own cup tournament with Team A vs Team B. Set the date, the captains, the format. Invite players. Live leaderboard updates as scores come in.

▸ 1,745 COURSES, 13 COUNTRIES
From Mallorca to Belek, full coverage in Germany, Austria and Switzerland. Par and handicap per hole, community-curated and OSM-grounded. More added every week.

▸ LIVE LEADERBOARD
Realtime sync. Every entry visible to the whole flight instantly. No "wait, what's the score" mid-round.

▸ PLAYER STATS
Win rate, halved/won/lost, holes-won percentage — per profile and across every match you've played.

▸ DUELS WITH FRIENDS
Find players by @handle, send a friend request, challenge them on the spot. Build your rivalry.

▸ COMMUNITY
Live cup results, photos from the course, 19th-hole talk — all in our Discord community.

▸ AVAILABLE IN 5 LANGUAGES
English, Deutsch, Español, Français, 日本語. Auto-detected from your phone.

▸ PRIVACY
8-digit code by email — no password, no tracking ads, no data sold. Profile, friendships and tournaments only.

Made by Comms Connect GmbH in Munich, Germany.
```

- `set-age-rating.js` — setzt 4+
- `set-pricing-free.js` — setzt Free
- `wait-for-build.js` — pollt auf TestFlight-Build-Ready

Diese Listings können direkt durch die Scripts in App Store Connect eingespielt werden — die EN/DE-Texte oben sind die Source-of-Truth.
